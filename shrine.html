♡ his heart ♡

I love how gentle Itachi is, especially underneath how quiet and intimidating he can seem. He cares deeply about the things he believes in, and I love how passionate and devoted he is even though he rarely shows it loudly.

♡ everything about him is pretty ♡

I love his hair so much. It looks so long and silky, and I love the little ponytail. Somehow it manages to look masculine, elegant, and ridiculously cute at the same time. I also love his face lines because they make his design feel so unique and instantly recognizable. He is sooo handsome. ♡

♡ little things i adore ♡

I love that he is associated with crows. It suits him perfectly. I also love that he has a sweet tooth and likes dango. There is something so cute about someone with his serious, mysterious aura loving sweets. I love his Akatsuki and ANBU outfits too. He always looks elegant and edgy at the same time.

♡ itachi & izumi ♡

One of the things that means the most to me is how deeply he loved Izumi. Even in such a tragic situation, he wanted to give her a beautiful life with him and something peaceful and full of love. I love their softer moments too, especially the thought of them sharing dango together. ♡

♡ gifts i would give him ♡

I would give him little crow plushies and homemade dishes to enjoy after his missions, with dango on the side of course. I love the idea of giving him something soft, comforting, and made with love. ♡

♡ why he is special to me ♡

He is so special to me because he is gentle, passionate, quiet, handsome, and so well written. I relate to how quiet he is, and I love that we are both commonly typed as INFJs. He is a complicated character who made terrible choices for reasons he believed were right, and that complexity makes him unforgettable to me. I love him so much. ♡
